# Livestock Rent

## The 20,000 USDC Founding Pilot Opportunity

**A hybrid founding-angel investment and real-asset pilot for a Web3-native investor**

**20 July 2026 | Brazil-first | Private concept brief — not an offer or solicitation**

> Brazil finishes one of the world's largest cattle herds inside a **R$ 249.5
> billion** value chain — the country's second-largest agricultural chain,
> behind only soybean — and finances almost none of it with instruments a
> global investor can actually hold. The rails already exist: CPRs registered
> at B3, CRAs issued by CVM-authorized securitization companies, official
> SISBOV identity on every traceable animal. Nobody has connected those rails
> to onchain capital. **Livestock Rent is building that connection, and this
> pilot is the first proof that it works.**

Livestock Rent is building the operating and technology layer for a new
category of agricultural RWA: professionally managed cattle, individually
traced through Brazil's official systems, financed through instruments the
Brazilian capital market already trusts, and administered through permissioned
Web3 infrastructure with native-USDC settlement.

The proposed 20,000 USDC founding commitment gives an early investor exposure
to two different value-creation engines at once:

1. **Long-term venture upside** from the Livestock Rent platform.
2. **Short-cycle operating proof** through the first CPR-financed cattle
   cohort and its CRA-based payment cycle.

This is not an investment in cattle. It is an opportunity to help create the
origination, servicing, data, compliance, and distribution infrastructure that
can finance many future herds — and to watch the first complete cycle execute
from the inside.

The two allocations are documented separately. The angel tranche is intended
to receive startup equity, a SAFE, or an equivalent long-term instrument
selected by counsel — long-term, illiquid, with no redemption claim after four
months. The CRA tranche participates in a legally established issuance; it may
be a founding allocation within a larger host CRA rather than a standalone
issuance, which is the economically rational path at pilot scale given the
fixed costs of securitization.

The investor is therefore not forced to choose between venture upside and
regulated fixed-income exposure. The same commitment builds the company **and**
finances the cohort that proves the asset engine — the proof that reprices the
platform.

### Why the coupon is deliberately conservative

A Brazil-literate allocator will immediately note that the illustrative
four-month coupon annualizes below the current Selic benchmark. That is by
design. The pilot coupon is priced **to be paid, not to impress**. We are not
asking the founding investor to underwrite yield; we are asking them to watch a
full cycle execute: capital in, cattle individually identified, CPRs registered
at B3, collections into the segregated estate, waterfall calculated, payment
settled, investor report published. **The return that matters from this tranche
is the proof — and the proof is what reprices the equity tranche.** Future
pools will be priced against realized loss data, realized FX costs, and the
full local-benchmark comparison, on a net-BRL and net-USD basis side by side.

### Tax treatment, stated plainly

Brazilian law currently exempts qualifying CRA remuneration from income tax
for **individual investors** under Article 3, IV of Law 11,033. This exemption
does not automatically extend to corporate vehicles, investment funds, or all
non-resident investors; their treatment depends on entity type, domicile, and
investment route. The structure is designed to **preserve the statutory
exemption where the investor and transaction qualify** — and the term sheet
process includes an investor-by-investor tax analysis before any commitment.
No universal "tax-free" claim is made.

## Why Livestock Rent can become a category leader

### 1. Brazil already has the productive asset

Brazil is one of the world's most important cattle economies. The opportunity
is not to invent demand for livestock; it is to make livestock financing more
transparent, auditable, programmable, and accessible to qualified global
capital.

The latest official data makes the scale concrete. Brazil's Ministry of
Agriculture and Livestock (Mapa) estimates the 2026 Gross Value of Agricultural
Production (VBP), using preliminary information available through May 2026:

| Mapa 2026 market signal | Preliminary estimate |
|---|---:|
| **Brazilian agricultural VBP** | **R$ 1.4 trillion** |
| **Livestock VBP** | **R$ 511.1 billion** |
| **Cattle VBP** | **R$ 249.5 billion** |
| **Cattle share of national VBP** | **~17.8%** |

Cattle is the second-largest agricultural value chain in Brazil, behind only
soybean, and the largest livestock chain in Mapa's published ranking. The
addressable production base is not the constraint — the constraint is
converting a carefully selected fraction of this quarter-trillion-real economy
into auditable, financeable CPR cash flows capable of supporting regulated CRA
products. That conversion layer is exactly what Livestock Rent builds.

The scale is especially attractive in a margin-sensitive year. High farm-gate
revenue does not guarantee a profitable cattle spread; it increases the value
of disciplined acquisition, feed, health, price, evidence, and cash controls.
Discipline is the product.

Beef and dairy generate cash through different operating cycles, giving the
platform a path to multiple products: closed beef-finishing vintages,
productive dairy-herd pools, livestock receivables financing, white-label
agricultural credit products, and data and servicing infrastructure for
lenders, securitizers, insurers, and asset managers.

The initial pilot keeps beef and dairy economics separately measured. A
rapid-cycle beef-finishing cohort is the first validation strategy; any dairy
animals remain in a separately underwritten sub-pool.

Santa Catarina and Rio Grande do Sul are the preferred first operating
environment because of their extension-service data, traceability
capabilities, and manageable pilot networks. After the model is proven, Mato
Grosso, Minas Gerais, and São Paulo — Brazil's three largest state-level
agricultural value pools — provide the natural expansion path.

### 2. Brazil already has institutional financial rails

Livestock Rent connects cattle operations to infrastructure Brazilian capital
markets already understand:

- **CPR — Cédula de Produto Rural:** an agricultural credit instrument used to
  document eligible livestock obligations.
- **B3:** Brazil's principal financial-market infrastructure, where the
  relevant CPR and CRA records are registered or deposited.
- **CRA — Certificado de Recebíveis do Agronegócio:** an
  agribusiness-receivables security issued by a CVM-authorized securitization
  company through a segregated securitization estate.

The intended path is:

```text
10,000 USDC CRA allocation
    -> lawful BRL conversion and CRA subscription
    -> securitization estate (CVM-authorized issuer)
    -> eligible livestock CPRs registered at B3
    -> cattle acquisition, feed, and management
    -> CPR collections
    -> CRA waterfall
    -> scheduled CRA payment
    -> authorized conversion and USDC settlement
```

The 10,000 USDC angel allocation does not pay for a standalone CRA issuance —
nor should it. It is catalytic capital: it validates the structure, prepares
the documentation and technology, engages the securitization company, fiduciary
agent, and other regulated partners, and makes the platform ready to originate
eligible CPRs at scale. The CRA allocation then participates as a founding
position within a legally established issuance.

The legal source of the scheduled CRA payment is the CRA and its
securitization waterfall, supported by collections on the assigned CPRs. B3
registration creates the institutional record and traceability of the
instruments; the livestock evidence system connects the financial backing to
the operating assets, animal by animal.

### 3. Every animal becomes an auditable data asset

Each admitted animal receives a role-restricted digital identity — implemented
as a non-transferable NFT — connected one-to-one to its official SISBOV
registration. Think of it as a verifiable-credential registry for biological
collateral, not a collectible.

The identity token becomes the animal's tamper-evident lifecycle index,
connecting physical identification; purchase invoice and farm entry; GTA
animal-movement records; health, vaccination, veterinary, and weight events;
feed and operating-performance data; independent physical attestations; CPR
and pool allocation; and sale, slaughter, death, loss, or retirement.

The token does not replace legal ownership or prove existence by itself. Its
commercial power comes from reconciling blockchain records with official
Brazilian data, physical inspections, financial documents, and independently
verified operating events — at a servicing cost per head that spreadsheet-based
collateral monitoring cannot match.

### 4. Why onchain at all

USDC is the distribution rail, not the source of the investment right. That
separation is the design, not a limitation. **Brazilian law does the
perfection; the chain does the distribution.**

Permissioned smart contracts support investor eligibility and transfer
controls; transparent pool-level accounting; reconciliation between asset
records and investor rights; auditable distribution calculations; wallet
allowlisting and recovery controls; and settlement in native USDC through
authorized providers.

The practical consequence: a CRA that today reaches a limited set of domestic
buyers can reach qualified global capital with near-zero per-investor servicing
cost, 24/7 settlement, and an audit trail that reconciles to the individual
animal rather than to a quarterly PDF. **The legal structure is deliberately
boring so the distribution can be radical.**

### 5. A credible path to an international credit rating

Animal-level transparency is necessary but not sufficient for institutional
capital in the United States, Europe, Japan, Singapore, and Australia. Most of
that capital operates under mandates that require a rating from a globally
recognized agency — and Fitch Ratings is formally recognized in every target
market: NRSRO with the SEC, ESMA-registered ECAI in the EU, recognized by the
JFSA in Japan, accepted by the MAS in Singapore, and an APRA-recognized ECAI
in Australia. A Fitch rating converts "trust our transparency" into "verify
with the agency your regulator already accepts."

The rating strategy is sequenced with scale, because rating fees are a fixed
cost that only volume can absorb:

| Phase | Scale | Rating action |
|---|---|---|
| Founding pilot (this document) | ~USD 10k CRA participation | **Unrated.** The 120-day cycle produces the rating dossier: asset-level performance data, documented servicing procedures, legal opinions |
| Pool 2 | R$ 5–20 million | Fitch **national-scale** structured finance rating (`sf(bra)`) — unlocks Fiagros and domestic institutional distribution |
| Pool 3+ | R$ 50 million+ | Fitch **international-scale** rating alongside the national one — unlocks foreign institutional mandates |

Two facts should be stated plainly. First, Brazil's sovereign rating at Fitch
is BB (stable) with a BB+ country ceiling, so an international-scale rating on
a BRL-collections CRA will be high yield, not investment grade — the
structure's USDC settlement design addresses exactly the transfer-and-
convertibility risk that drives that ceiling, and mitigants such as offshore
reserve accounts or pre-funded coupons can be evaluated at Pool 3 scale.
Second, the pilot's animal-level evidence is not marketing: default,
mortality, recovery, and cost data reconciled per head is precisely the input
Fitch's structured finance methodology requires for pulverized collateral
pools — and it is the input no competing livestock originator currently has.
The 120-day pilot is, in practice, the first vintage of that dataset.

## Deployment of the first 10,000 USDC: build the company

The Founding Angel Investment finances the minimum platform needed to
originate and service the first institutional-grade livestock asset.

### Physical and operating readiness

- prepare the initial farm infrastructure and cattle-receiving process;
- establish identification, weighing, handling, feed, health, and inventory
  procedures;
- implement controlled evidence capture for every admitted animal;
- define farm, veterinarian, verifier, and offtaker responsibilities; and
- establish bank, accounting, and operational reconciliation controls.

### Technology and tokenization

- build the SISBOV-connected animal identity registry;
- implement role-based minting and lifecycle updates;
- create the off-chain evidence store and onchain document hashes;
- build the pool dashboard and reconciliation workflow;
- configure investor permissioning and USDC distribution logic; and
- test pause, dispute, stale-data, signer-revocation, and recovery procedures.

### Financial and regulatory structuring

- validate the farm agreement and livestock ownership model;
- prepare the first CPR documentation and B3 registration workflow;
- engage the CVM-authorized securitization company and design the CRA path
  with qualified partners;
- define investor eligibility, KYC/AML, custody, reporting, and transfer
  rules — including the offering route and registration analysis for any
  non-resident participation;
- prepare the pool documentation and operating data room to the requirements
  of a global rating agency's structured finance methodology (Fitch),
  including stress scenarios for cattle price, feed cost, and mortality;
- validate the BRL-to-USDC conversion and payment process with authorized
  providers; and
- prepare the operating data room for future institutional capital.

### Proposed angel-investor position

Subject to the final term sheet and legal advice, the founding investor can be
offered startup equity, a SAFE, or an equivalent long-term participation
instrument; founding-investor economics determined before the next priced
round; periodic access to operating, product, and fundraising updates;
priority access to evaluate future livestock pools; and participation in
strategic product-design discussions.

This tranche is venture capital. It is invested to increase the enterprise
value of Livestock Rent over the long term and is not expected to return
capital or generate liquidity within the first 120 days.

## Deployment of the second 10,000 USDC: CRA-backed asset exposure

The second allocation is a subscription to, or permissioned participation in,
a CRA backed by eligible livestock CPRs. The CPR proceeds finance productive
assets and their direct operating inputs: cattle acquisition; feed, pasture
supplements, and nutrition; identification and initial veterinary procedures;
direct transportation and admission costs where applicable; and a limited
operating reserve for the first cohort.

Every animal financed through the underlying CPRs is individually identified,
documented, reconciled, and allocated only once. The animal-level evidence
supports monitoring and servicing; the investor's enforceable financial rights
remain the rights defined by the CRA.

Collections on the assigned CPRs enter the securitization waterfall, which
pays expenses and required reserves before CRA remuneration and principal
according to the final issuance documents. Settlement may be converted to USDC
through authorized providers after the BRL obligation is calculated and paid
through the lawful structure. The CPR payment dates, cattle operating cycle,
reserves, and expected offtake are underwritten together so the structure does
not depend on a forced cattle sale at an uneconomic price.

### The rapid-validation objective

The pilot demonstrates, within approximately 120 days, that Livestock Rent can:

1. originate a real livestock cohort;
2. deploy investor capital into verified productive assets;
3. trace every animal through SISBOV-linked evidence;
4. measure biological and financial performance;
5. control operating cash and supporting documents;
6. originate and register eligible CPRs at B3;
7. assign the CPRs to the securitization estate;
8. reconcile qualifying collections to the CRA waterfall;
9. execute the scheduled investor report and payment; and
10. produce the first vintage of asset-level performance data in the format a
    global rating agency's structured finance methodology requires.

## Proposed 120-day execution roadmap

### Days 0–30: make the platform operational

- finalize the two investment instruments and segregate both allocations;
- select the first farm, veterinarian, verifier, and expected exit channel;
- approve the cattle-acquisition and feed budget;
- deploy the initial identity-token and evidence workflow;
- configure the accounting and investor-reporting process; and
- finalize the eligible CPR, B3 registration, assignment, CRA, reserve, and
  waterfall implementation with the securitization partners.

### Days 31–60: deploy the livestock capital

- acquire the first cattle;
- validate invoices, SISBOV status, physical tags, health, and farm admission;
- mint one controlled identity token per accepted animal;
- record baseline weight, condition, location, and expected operating plan;
- begin feed, health, and performance monitoring;
- issue and register the eligible livestock CPRs at B3; and
- assign the CPRs to the CRA securitization estate.

### Days 61–120: produce evidence, revenue, and the first investor report

- reconcile animals, operating events, invoices, and cash every month;
- publish cohort performance and exception metrics;
- test transfer, mortality, health, missing-data, and retirement workflows;
- collect CPR payments and operating proceeds according to the documented
  schedule;
- reconcile collections, costs, reserves, and CRA obligations;
- calculate the CRA waterfall and investor payment; and
- execute the scheduled CRA payment, settling in USDC if legally and
  operationally permitted.

## Why 20,000 USDC can be catalytic

The initial commitment is intentionally focused. It is enough to move
Livestock Rent from thesis to evidence:

```text
10,000 USDC equity + 10,000 USDC CRA participation
    -> operating, tokenization, and securitization infrastructure
    -> B3-registered CPRs financing an identified livestock cohort
    -> verified performance data and CPR collections
    -> CRA waterfall and the scheduled four-month payment
    -> first complete investor proof
    -> stronger institutional fundraising position
```

One successful cohort creates more than a single return event. It creates a
documented underwriting methodology; a verified farm and service-provider
network; animal-level performance history; evidence for regulated partners;
proof for future Web3 and agribusiness investors; a repeatable origination and
servicing process; and the foundation for larger beef, dairy, and white-label
credit pools.

The pilot converts capital into the most valuable early-stage asset the
company can own: **credible operating proof.**

## The investor's two upside engines

### Venture upside

The startup tranche participates in the potential growth of origination and
structuring fees; recurring livestock servicing and management fees;
performance participation tied to realized results; traceability and
collateral-monitoring software; white-label RWA infrastructure; proprietary
livestock-underwriting data — the direct input for future agency ratings and
an asset no competing originator holds; and future regional and international
distribution.

### CRA-backed asset proof

The CRA tranche gives the investor regulated fixed-income exposure backed by
the first eligible livestock CPRs — and makes the investor part of the
company's proof-of-execution story.

The founding investor gets to evaluate, from primary evidence: whether capital
reached verified cattle; whether animal and financial records reconciled;
whether the CPRs were registered at B3 and validly assigned; whether the farm
met biological-performance expectations; whether operating costs remained
controlled; whether CPR collections reached the securitization estate; whether
the CRA waterfall and remuneration were transparent; and whether the model
deserves additional capital.

This combination is unusually powerful for an early-stage Web3 investor: the
startup investment funds the platform, while the CRA allocation creates
regulated fixed-income evidence that can reprice the platform.

## Why now

- Institutional interest in real-world assets is moving beyond tokenized cash
  and government securities toward differentiated credit and productive
  assets — and the institutional product gap is in exactly this middle ground.
- Brazil combines a large livestock economy with mature agricultural credit
  instruments and increasingly digital traceability.
- Mapa estimates Brazilian cattle VBP at R$ 249.5 billion in 2026 — the
  country's second-largest agricultural value chain and a deep origination
  universe.
- SISBOV identity plus animal-level digital credentials can set a new standard
  for auditable biological collateral.
- B3, CPRs, and CRAs give the project a path to scale through infrastructure
  already recognized — and required — by Brazilian capital markets.
- Stablecoin adoption makes global settlement practical, while permissioning
  and regulated partners preserve compliance under Brazil's 2026
  virtual-asset framework.
- The rating infrastructure for agribusiness receivables is mature and
  battle-tested: Fitch acted on 191 CRA series in a single review in June
  2026. What the market lacks is an originator producing pulverized,
  asset-level performance data worth rating — the gap this platform fills.
- The team that builds the verified operating dataset first becomes the
  trusted originator and servicer for an entire category of livestock RWAs.
